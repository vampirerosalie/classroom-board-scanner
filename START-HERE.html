Camera & quality tips

Use the supplied board layout, flat and facing the phone. Write large with a thick black marker. Keep one board in view. Move closer when asked. Quality checks are approximate and can miss glare, overlap or a misaligned crop. Review every saved image.

A live camera needs HTTPS, or localhost on the same device. A plain local-network HTTP address will usually block the camera. The photo option also works for testing. Keep this tab visible while scanning.
